I am a physicist and science teacher. My academic work focuses on fluid mechanics (in particular microfluidics, and submesoscale ocean dynamics). Currently, I teach physics at Melbourne Central Catholic High School in Melbourne, FL.

This site collects information about my research, teaching, academic work, and experiments that can be done without a fully equipped laboratory.

02 / ABOUT

About

I studied physics and mathematics at Worcester Polytechnic Institute before completing an Sc.M. in Physics at Brown University, along with the Sheridan Certificate in Teaching & Course Design.

My research experience spans computational ocean modeling, experimental microfluidics, and terahertz spectroscopy. I am interested in making physics understandable, experimentally approachable, and connected to the world outside the classroom.
